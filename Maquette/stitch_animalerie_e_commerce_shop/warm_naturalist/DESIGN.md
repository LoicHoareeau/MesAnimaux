## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, nature-inspired lifestyle aesthetic for a modern pet care retail experience. It pairs the heritage, care, and organic integrity of traditional apothecary and artisanal goods with the frictionless precision of contemporary e-commerce.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Earthy Sophistication:** Moving away from sterile pet clinical sites or neon toy discounters; spaces feel curated, gentle, and tactile.
- **Organic Warmth:** Driven by creamy undertones, grounded forest greens, and vibrant sunlit clay accents.
- **Trust & Clarity:** Clear typographic hierarchy, generous breathing room, structured content enclosures, and straightforward commerce cues.
- **Tactile Softness:** Subtly rounded geometry, soft ambient shadows, and pill-shaped badges that convey a welcoming, pet-safe emotional landscape.

## Colors

The palette balances forest-grown depth with baked terracotta energy and rich, oat-toned neutrals.

- **Primary (`#1b4332` - Deep Forest Green):** Anchors navigation, prime actions, hero statements, and heavy editorial headers. Evokes health, nature, and veterinary trustworthiness.
- **Secondary (`#e76f51` - Warm Terracotta):** Directs user attention to primary conversion steps, notification badges, active states, cart counters, and promotional tags.
- **Tertiary (`#d4a373` - Honey Amber):** Used for ratings, nutritional highlights, subtle callout borders, and warm decorative accents.
- **Neutral Core (`#2b2a27` - Dark Roasted Umber):** Replaces pure black to maintain an organic, warm reading contrast across typography and structural borders.
- **Surface Roles:**
  - **Canvas Base:** Soft Cream (`#faf8f5`)
  - **Surface Container / Cards:** Crisp Milk White (`#ffffff`) or Warm Sand Recess (`#f4efe6`)
  - **Subtle Dividers & Outlines:** Low-opacity warm taupe (`rgba(43, 42, 39, 0.08)`)

## Typography

The type system brings character and human craftsmanship into alignment with optimal mobile scanability.

- **Display & Headlines (Epilogue):** Provides warmth, structured geometric presence, and an artisanal magazine voice without appearing archaic. Used across product titles, curated collection features, and hero titles.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers clean readability, balanced apertures, and friendly rounded geometry across dense specifications (ingredients, pet weight brackets, shipping statuses, and checkout forms).
- **Styling Rules:** Ensure all numerical prices use tabular figures or bold weights of Plus Jakarta Sans for instant visual price processing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a responsive 12-column grid on desktop, scaling to a 6-column grid on tablet, and a 4-column fluid layout on mobile devices.

- **Desktop (min-width: 1024px):** Max container width capped at `1280px` centered with `3rem` lateral page margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Product grids typically present as 4-up or 3-up collections.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Fluid margins of `2rem` and `1.25rem` gutters with 2-up or 3-up product layouts.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Snug `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Horizontal scrolling carousels with peeked edges are preferred for collections (e.g., category badges, recent treats, dietary tags) to save vertical real estate.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Micro-gaps between text and labels strictly employ `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while internal container paddings run on `space-md` (mobile cards) and `space-lg` (desktop cards).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through gentle, warm tonal contrast and diffuse ambient drop shadows rather than stark elevation planes.

- **Level 0 (Flat Base):** Canvas background `#faf8f5`. Content zones alternate between base canvas and `#f4efe6` blocks for structural sectioning.
- **Level 1 (Crisp Surface):** Product cards, navigation bars, and filters use `#ffffff` backed by a tinted warm shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(43, 42, 39, 0.05)` and a crisp borderline `1px solid rgba(43, 42, 39, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Interactive Containers):** Raised cards on hover and bottom sticky sheets leverage `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Cart Drawer & Overlays):** Modal sheets and sliding mini-carts introduce an atmospheric backdrop scrim (`rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.25)` blurred with `4px` backdrop-filter) and a deep directional shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(43, 42, 39, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy is organic, soft, and approachable, avoiding harsh industrial angles to mirror pet-safe forms and playful character.

- **Base Cards & Surfaces:** Scaled at `0.5rem` (8px) on small elements and `1rem` (16px) on product cards and major containers to maintain structural order without sharp corners.
- **Badges, Filter Pills, and Buttons:** Use fully pill-shaped radii (`9999px`) to emphasize touch-friendliness and visual softness.
- **Product Image Frames:** Wrapped in soft `rounded-lg` masks with a `3:4` or `1:1` aspect ratio, slightly inset with a `1px` subtle warm outline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Forest green (`#1b4332`) fill, cream white text, pill-shaped (`9999px`), `12px 24px` padding. Transitions on hover to `#255943` with a subtle `translateY(-1px)` lift.
- **Secondary:** Transparent fill, `1.5px` border in `#1b4332`, dark umber text, pill-shaped.
- **Accent / Instant Checkout:** Terracotta (`#e76f51`) fill with white text, utilized for time-sensitive conversion triggers ("Subscribe & Save", "Add to Cart").

### Badges & Chips
- **Product Category Chips:** Soft cream `#f4efe6` background with `#2b2a27` text; turns to `#1b4332` with white text when selected.
- **Dietary & Animal Attributes:** Pill badges (e.g., "Grain-Free", "Hypoallergenic", "Puppy") rendered in muted tints (e.g., Terracotta 10% tint `#fdf0ed` with `#e76f51` text).

### Product Cards
- Clean pure white (`#ffffff`) background bordered by subtle warm taupe outline.
- Includes floating top-right favorite heart button, top-left dietary pill badge, product image, brand line, bold title in Epilogue, price display, and an immediate quick-add button.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Warm white surface `#ffffff`, `1px` border in `rgba(43, 42, 39, 0.15)`, `0.5rem` border radius. Focus ring glows with `0 0 0 3px rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.15)` and border color shifts to `#1b4332`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded square (`4px`) and circular toggles filling with `#1b4332` and white checkmarks when active.

### E-Commerce Cart Badge & Persistent State
- **Persistent Header Cart Trigger:** Displays an artisanal tote/bag icon accompanied by a high-contrast pill counter floating on top-right in secondary terracotta (`#e76f51`) with bold white numeral (`label-sm`).
- **Cart State Indicators:**
  - Empty state displays friendly pet illustration and warm editorial copy.
  - Active state triggers an amber free-shipping progress tracker bar (`#d4a373`) atop the slide-out cart drawer.
  - Mobile bottom navigation anchors a persistent condensed checkout bar whenever items exist in the bag, featuring item count, running subtotal, and an immediate `#e76f51` CTA button.
