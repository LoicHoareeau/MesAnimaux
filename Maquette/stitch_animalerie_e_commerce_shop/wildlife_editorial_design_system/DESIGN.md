## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, nature-first editorial experience inspired by avian wildlife documentation and contemporary ecological storytelling. It bridges the intellectual prestige of high-end biological periodicals with the clarity of modern digital design.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Reverent & Natural:** Rooted in botanical greens, raw linen textures, and the serene stillness of untouched ecosystems.
- **Editorial Distinction:** Large-scale typographies, measured white spaces, and photographic centerpieces evoke the intimacy of high-craft monographs.
- **Quiet Precision:** Utilitarian labels, fine architectural borders, and deliberate typographic rhythm cultivate trust and scientific authority without clinical detachment.

### Design Movement
The aesthetic synthesizes **Minimalist Editorial** with **Organic Tactility**. Surfaces borrow the softness of natural papers (`#FAF8F5`), punctuated by deep forest canopies (`#183A27`), subtle vegetable tones (`#2D5A3F`), and quiet sage accents (`#A3B899`).

## Colors

The palette derives from dense canopy forests, wet moss, pale reeds, and unbleached fiber. Contrast is calibrated to mimic fine matte print on tinted stock.

### Palette Composition
- **Primary (`#183A27` - Forest Green):** The commanding dark tone for primary headers, high-emphasis action buttons, and dominant structural frames.
- **Secondary (`#2D5A3F` - Moss Green):** Used for interactive states, supportive section titles, and active indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#A3B899` - Sage):** An organic highlight used for quiet tags, decorative borders, active pills, and botanical accents.
- **Neutral Foreground (`#19201B` - Deep Charcoal):** The primary reading ink for body copy, micro-labels, and crisp iconography.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FAF8F5` - Alabaster Cream):** The underlying foundation that replaces stark digital white with a warm, natural pulp feel.
- **Surface Substrate (`#F4EFEA` - Warm Sand/Oat):** Card fills, input container surfaces, and segmented controls.

### Semantic Rules
- Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) is reserved exclusively for elevated card plates, modal windows, or over-image badges to preserve dramatic photographic pop.
- Pure black (`#000000`) is never used; `#19201B` maintains ink depth without harsh synthetic contrast.

## Typography

The pairing juxtaposes **Newsreader** (an authoritative transitional serif) with **Manrope** (a balanced, contemporary geometric sans-serif).

### Hierarchy & Style Nuances
- **Newsreader for Headings:** Titling mimics curated exhibition plates and natural history monographs. Headings feature tight line-heights (1.05 to 1.15) and slight negative letter tracking to build dense, poetic header blocks.
- **Manrope for Structure & Body:** Ensures crisp legibility across data tags, descriptive accounts, and interactive states.
- **Micro-labels (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em`) to act as taxonomic identifiers, coordinates, species classification tags, and status legends.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model combines a disciplined 12-column editorial grid with dynamic breathing room, prioritizing generous visual pauses around wildlife photography.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout; outer margin `margin` (`1.25rem`), column gap `gutter` (`1.5rem`). Content stacks vertically with full-bleed photographic hero options.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout; margin `2.5rem`, gutter `1.75rem`. Cards shift into staggered pairs.
- **Desktop (>= 1025px):** 12-column fluid grid locked to a max width of `1440px`; outer margin `margin-desktop` (`4rem`), gutter `gutter-desktop` (`2.5rem`).

### Spacing Rhythm
Vertical margins leverage large leaps (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) between narrative sections to simulate page turns in an art book, while intra-component gaps (`space-xs` through `space-md`) maintain dense, functional association.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids high-gloss synthetic drop shadows, relying instead on **tactile tonal layering** and **fine hairline boundaries**.

### Spatial Strategy
- **Layer 0 (Canvas):** `#FAF8F5` Alabaster Cream base.
- **Layer 1 (Substrate Containers):** `#F4EFEA` Warm Sand/Oat for grouped panels and data shelves.
- **Layer 2 (Plates & Modals):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces with an ultra-diffused atmospheric shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(25, 32, 27, 0.06)`. The deep charcoal tint roots the shadow naturally into the cream base.
- **Borders & Rules:** Crisp `1px` lines utilizing `#183A27` at `10%` to `15%` opacity (`rgba(24, 58, 39, 0.12)`) define edges without visual weight.
- **Glass & Frosting:** Header bars and contextual image controls utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over a `rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.82)` veil.

## Shapes

The geometry balances organic gentleness with classical architectural structure.

### Radius Scale
- **Base Components (Inputs, interactive buttons, standard cards):** `0.5rem` (`roundedness = 2`).
- **Feature Surfaces (Photographic cards, hero framing, editorial spotlights):** `1rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Floating Badges, Status Chips, Micro Pills:** Fully rounded / capsule form (`9999px`).
- **Photography Masks:** Clean soft corners (`0.75rem` to `1.25rem`) to maintain edge sharpness while eliminating digital harshness.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Button:** Solid Forest Green (`#183A27`) background with warm cream text (`#FAF8F5`). Fully rounded pill or soft radius (`0.5rem`), padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Subtle hover transition shifting background to `#2D5A3F`.
- **Secondary / Outline Button:** Transparent fill, `1px` border in `#183A27`, text in `#183A27`. Hover fills subtly with `rgba(45, 90, 63, 0.06)`.
- **Ghost Action:** Manrope medium text with an arrow glyph (`→`), underlined by a hairline rule that expands on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Editorial Category Badges:** Small uppercase text (`label-caps`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, pill-shaped. Background `#F4EFEA` with deep charcoal text (`#19201B`).
- **Conservation Status Badges:** Muted botanical indicator dots (e.g., `#2D5A3F` for Stable, earthy ochre for Vulnerable) flanked by a semi-transparent pill container (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)` on images).

### Cards & Photography Displays
- **Specimen & Wildlife Cards:** Flat `#FFFFFF` or `#F4EFEA` base, framed by a delicate `1px` border (`rgba(24, 58, 39, 0.08)`). Top area hosts high-aspect ratio wildlife photography (3:2 or 4:5) with `rounded-md` internal margins. Bottom area hosts species taxonomy: Newsreader headline, botanical family tag in `label-caps`, and concise field notes in `body-sm`.
- **Hero Exhibition Plate:** Full-width photographic backdrop layered with a progressive linear gradient (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(24, 58, 39, 0.1) 0%, rgba(24, 58, 39, 0.85) 100%)`), overlaid with display-hero typography in off-white.

### Form Inputs & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#F4EFEA`, border `1px solid rgba(24, 58, 39, 0.15)`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`. Focus state tightens the outline to `1.5px solid #183A27` without neon glow.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Deep forest green fill when active with an ivory check mark. Inactive state features an uncolored center bordered in `#A3B899`.

### Observation & Data Panels
- **Field Metric Counter:** Large Newsreader numerals paired with small sans-serif descriptive keys below, separated by thin vertical dividers (`rgba(24, 58, 39, 0.1)`).
