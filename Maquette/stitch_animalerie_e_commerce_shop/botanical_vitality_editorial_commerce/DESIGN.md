## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes deep botanical naturalism with luminous, sun-drenched commercial vitality. It marries the stately poise of natural history monographs—monumental serif typography, contemplative negative space, and deep forest undergrowth—with the kinetic warmth, conversion intent, and approachable joy of modern companion-animal commerce.

### Brand Personality & Tone
- **Botanical Prestige & Care:** Grounded in ancient arboreal greens and unbleached organic substrates, projecting scientific integrity and thoughtful animal wellness.
- **Sunlit Radiance:** Warm amber and golden saffron highlights punctuate the experience, injecting optimism, domestic comfort, and joyful vitality.
- **Editorial E-Commerce:** Clean geometric layouts pair with generous imagery and exquisite italic display titling, establishing an aura of luxury pet nourishment without clinical aloofness.

### Design Movement
An intentional synthesis of **Minimalist Editorial** and **Modern Warm Commerce**. Tactile matte finishes, linen-toned surfaces, and hairline architectural dividers replace synthetic plastic gloss, while high-contrast golden accents guide discovery and interaction.

## Colors

The chromatic palette bridges dense understory canopies with radiant solar accents and raw paper textures. 

### Palette Role Breakdown
- **Primary (`#133322` - Botanical Forest Green):** The principal brand anchor, deployed on primary titles, deep headers, key interactive triggers, and confident editorial containers.
- **Secondary (`#D99B00` - Radiant Warm Amber):** The energetic commerce driver. Powers high-conversion CTAs, sale callouts, basket notifications, and promotional banners.
- **Tertiary (`#3D644D` - Verdant Pine):** Supporting botanical shade for hover transitions, secondary pill states, filter indicators, and subtle line art.
- **Surface Foundations (`#FBF9F5` & `#F3EEE5`):** Pure stark digital white is replaced by warm linen and oatmeal substrates, giving screens the comforting texture of heavy cotton paper.
- **Reading Ink (`#1A221D`):** Rich vegetal charcoal, delivering optimal contrast against cream backgrounds without digital harshness.

### Application Rules
- **The Solar Balance:** Gold (`#D99B00` / `#E5A912`) is strictly an intentional spark—never used for long-form reading backgrounds or multi-paragraph text. It brings energy to announcements, conversion triggers, active tabs, and review stars.
- **Contrast Integrity:** High-contrast text on deep forest containers utilizes pure `#FFFFFF` or `#FBF9F5`. When text rests on saffron gold, use `#133322` or `#0D2418` for accessibility and impact.

## Typography

Typography balances historical literary refinement with high-density commercial legibility.

### Expressive Headings
- **Newsreader:** Employs delicate transitional serifs for narrative immersion. Display and hero levels leverage generous, organic italics—particularly for single qualifying words within headings (e.g., *Feline*, *Botanical*, *Nourishment*) to capture editorial poetry.
- **Tight Headings Tracking:** Headlines employ negative letter spacing (`-0.025em` to `-0.01em`) and compact line heights to craft cohesive typographical focal points.

### Utilitarian Body & Data
- **Manrope:** Serves as the high-clarity structural workhorse for product specifications, nutritional panels, pricing tiers, and interface microcopy.
- **Category & Origin Labels:** `label-caps` operates in all-caps with generous tracking (`0.08em`), establishing clean taxonomy across dietary classifications, animal breeds, and certification tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial rhythm draws on balanced publication layouts, interspersing dense product grids with generous visual sanctuaries.

### Grid Architecture
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` margins and gutters. Single-column stacked narratives; 2-column compact catalog cards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` outer padding, providing balanced spacing for split hero modules and 3-column product cards.
- **Desktop (>= 1025px):** 12-column responsive grid with a strict `1440px` maximum viewport container. Margins expand to `4rem` (`margin-desktop`) with `2.5rem` gutters (`gutter-desktop`).

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Editorial Breathers:** Narrative breaks between content chapters utilize large vertical spacing tokens (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) to establish cadence.
- **Component Clustering:** Intra-card information, badges, and action rows cluster tightly (`space-xs` through `space-md`), keeping transactional flows unambiguous and ergonomic.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids synthetic glowing drop shadows in favor of **warm tonal layering** and **natural hairline contours**.

### Depth Layers
- **Base Canvas (Layer 0):** `#FBF9F5` Cream Linen substrate.
- **Recessed Inset (Layer -1):** `#F3EEE5` Oatmeal Sand for announcement ribbons, technical parameter tables, and input wells.
- **Floating Containers (Layer 1):** `#FFFFFF` card plates framed with a fine, translucent hairline: `1px solid rgba(19, 51, 34, 0.08)`.
- **Active Elevation (Hover / Modals):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces paired with a diffused, natural forest-tinted shadow: `0 16px 36px -10px rgba(19, 51, 34, 0.09)`.

### Architectural Borders
Divisions between structural content columns rely on subtle `1px` borders tinted with `rgba(19, 51, 34, 0.12)`, mirroring traditional printing rules.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary unifies architectural precision with organic softness.

### Radius System
- **Structural Cards, Inputs, and Panels:** Formed with a measured `0.5rem` radius (`roundedness: 2`), reflecting modern bespoke packaging.
- **Hero Containers & Visual Vignettes:** Broadened to `1rem` (`rounded-lg`) or `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`) to soften photographic animal portraits.
- **Pills, CTAs, and Badges:** Rendered as full pills (`9999px`) to invite touch and reinforce the friendly warmth of companion-animal care.

## Components

### Top Announcement Bar
- **Surface:** Solid Golden Saffron (`#E5A912`) or Deep Forest (`#133322`).
- **Typography:** `label-caps` in deep ink (`#133322`) when saffron, or warm cream (`#FBF9F5`) when forest.
- **Content:** Shipping incentives, seasonal botanical harvest notices, or membership perks centered with subtle inline icons.

### Buttons & Commerce Triggers
- **Primary Conversion CTA (Add to Basket / Buy):** Saffron Gold fill (`#D99B00`), deep forest text (`#133322`), font weight 600, fully rounded pill (`9999px`), padding `0.875rem 2rem`. Hover darkens smoothly to `#C98E00`.
- **Editorial Brand Button:** Deep Forest Green (`#133322`) fill with cream text (`#FBF9F5`). Hover transitions to `#3D644D`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill, `1.5px` border in `#133322`, text in `#133322`. Hover fills with subtle `#F3EEE5`.
- **Text Link:** Newsreader italic or Manrope medium, paired with an expanding underline and directional arrow (`→`).

### Product & Editorial Cards
- **Product Commerce Card:** Clean `#FFFFFF` or `#F3EEE5` shell, `1px solid rgba(19, 51, 34, 0.08)` border, `rounded-md`. Upper zone hosts edge-to-edge animal/product lifestyle photography with an inset botanical badge (`#133322` or `#E5A912`). Lower zone features Newsreader item title, dietary descriptor in `body-sm`, price highlight in bold sans-serif, and a discrete quick-add circular button.
- **Curated Ingredient Card:** Cream background, delicate pencil illustration or specimen macro-photo, followed by biological name in `label-caps` and health benefits in `body-sm`.

### Chips, Pills & Tags
- **Dietary & Eco Badges:** Capsule form (`9999px`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`. Neutral variant: `#F3EEE5` fill with `#133322` text. Botanical variant: `#133322` fill with `#FBF9F5` text. Vitality variant: `#FEF7E6` fill with `#D99B00` text and border `1px solid rgba(217, 155, 0, 0.3)`.

### Form Fields & Interactive Controls
- **Inputs:** Linen container `#FFFFFF` with border `1px solid rgba(19, 51, 34, 0.18)`, border radius `0.5rem`, padding `0.875rem 1rem`. Focus state tightens to `1.5px solid #133322` accompanied by a gentle `0 0 0 3px rgba(229, 169, 18, 0.25)` ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded inputs filled with Deep Forest (`#133322`) and an ivory tick mark upon selection. Inactive border in `#7F9C89`.
